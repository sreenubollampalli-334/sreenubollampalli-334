- ⏱️ Focus Timers, Daily Goals & Study Session History
- 📂 Private Resource Library & Access-Controlled File Sharing
- 🤖 Streaming AI Study Mentor powered by Spring AI

- 🧠 AI Flashcards with Spaced-Repetition Scheduling
- 🎯 Adaptive Learning & Topic Mastery Tracking
- 💡 Personalized Study Recommendations
- ✅ Human-in-the-Loop AI Study Plan Approval
- 🤝 Intelligent Study Partner Matching
- 💬 Real-Time Private Messaging
- 📖 Collaborative Study Rooms
- 👥 Course Communities & Discussion Forums
- 🗒️ Shared Notes & AI-Assisted Room Collaboration
- 📈 Learning Analytics & Progress Tracking
- 🏆 XP, Levels, Streaks & Achievements
- 🔔 Notifications & Scheduled Study Reminders
- 🛡️ Administrative Moderation, Auditing & Monitoring
- 📱 Responsive Across Desktop, Tablet & Mobile

### Tech Stack
